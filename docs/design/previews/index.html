Three directions for C.S.A.S

Each one is a complete answer to the same brief, not a variation on a theme. Open any of them to see the public home page, the agent's ticket queue and the customer conversation — in light and dark, in English and Arabic. Then pick one; step 10 turns it into tokens and a component library.

Operations Desk

An instrument for people who work a queue all day: dense rows, numbers you can scan without reading, and colour used only where it carries a state.

Open Operations Desk →

Dossier

A support ticket is a record, so the interface behaves like a well-kept file: warm paper, a serif for anything that is read rather than scanned, and rules instead of boxes.

Open Dossier →

Signal

The quiet, modern product: soft neutrals, one green that means resolved, and enough room that nothing feels urgent until something is.

Open Signal →
Colours come from docs/design/palettes.json and every pair is verified against WCAG 2.1 AA by npm run check:contrast. Fonts are bundled, not loaded from a CDN. No gradients, no glow, no blur, no emoji icons, no autoplaying anything — motion is opacity and transform only, and is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion.
